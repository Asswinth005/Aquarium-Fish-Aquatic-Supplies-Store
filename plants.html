Low-Tech Setup (Beginner Friendly)

No CO2 injection needed • Low to medium lighting

  • Best Species: Anubias, Java Fern, Cryptocoryne, Bucephalandra
  • Lighting: 15 – 30 PAR (Standard LED hood)
  • Fertilization: Liquid all-in-one dose 1x weekly

High-Tech Setup (Pro Aquascaper)

Pressurized CO2 (20-30ppm) • High RGB lighting

  • Best Species: Monte Carlo, Dwarf Hairgrass, Rotala Indica, Ludwigia
  • Lighting: 50 – 100+ PAR (Full spectrum WRGB light)
  • Fertilization: Daily NPK + Micro dosing + Aquasoil

Aquatic Horticulture

Plant Care & Propagation Masterclass

Learn how to trim, propagate, and maintain peak growth without algae issues.

Photoperiod & Light PAR

Run full-spectrum LEDs for 7-8 hours maximum with a timer. Excess lighting without nutrients causes green dust algae.

Target: 30-80 PAR

Micro & Macro Dosing

Stem plants feed via water column (Liquid NPK), while heavy root feeders (Amazon Swords, Crypts) require substrate Root Tabs every 3 months.

Target: 15-25 ppm NO3

Trimming & Re-Planting

Cut stem plants at node joints with curved aquascaping scissors. Re-insert the top cuttings into aquasoil to double your plant density!

Technique: Stem Propagation
