4-Step Live Fish Acclimation Protocol

Follow our proven step-by-step procedure to ensure zero stress and optimal health when introducing new fish to your tank.

1

Temperature Float

Float the sealed bag in your aquarium for 15-20 minutes to equalize water temperature gradually.

2

Drip Acclimation

Add 1/2 cup of tank water every 5 minutes for 45 minutes to adjust pH and water hardness smoothly.

3

Net Transfer Only

Gently net the fish into the tank. Never pour shipping bag water into your display tank.

4

Dim Lights & Observe

Keep aquarium lights off for 4 hours post-release to minimize stress and prevent aggression.
